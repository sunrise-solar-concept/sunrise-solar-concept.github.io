About us

Powering electric driving with the sun

Sunrise Panels is a proposed solar panel system for Tesla and other electric vehicles, in the automotive technology and renewable energy industry.

The company concept

The product uses solar energy to provide additional power that may support vehicle charging and reduce dependence on traditional charging stations. It focuses on three things: convenience, sustainability and energy independence.

What sets it apart is the focus on combining solar technology with electric vehicles specifically, with an emphasis on modern design and the EV experience.

Location: Canada. We sell online first, through our website and app, with showrooms proposed.

This website presents a college project. Sunrise Panels is not manufactured, approved or sold by Tesla, Inc.

Who it's for

EV owners and people thinking about buying one, especially Tesla owners.

We have in mind drivers who care about renewable energy, technology and automotive innovation: regular commuters, frequent travellers and anyone who wants more flexibility in how they charge.

Built with the right partners

The product would rely on photovoltaic solar cells, protective glass, wiring and connectors, mounting structures, electronic controllers and power-management parts, all made for outdoor automotive use.

We'd work with suppliers experienced in solar technology, automotive components, durability and quality control. No suppliers have been confirmed yet.

      Parked or driving?

      The concept includes generating solar energy while the vehicle is parked. Generating energy while driving would depend on whether the final design supports it.

      Either way, solar is meant to add to your normal charging, not replace it. How much it adds depends on panel area, how sunny it is, the weather and the vehicle's own energy use.

      No charging speeds or range figures are claimed here. They'll only be shared once the system has been designed and tested.

      Sustainability

      Cleaner energy for the way we move

      Electric vehicles are cleaner when the electricity behind them is clean. Solar on the car itself brings the energy source one step closer.

      Built to last outdoors

      A longer-lasting product means less waste. We'd choose protective glass and materials made for outdoor automotive use, and suppliers with a track record in durability and quality control.

      Professional installation by qualified technicians helps the system perform well and last, and the one-year warranty covers defects.

      Cancelling a pre-order

      You can cancel your pre-order at any time before your installation appointment. We'll refund the full amount you paid, including any discount-adjusted price, to your original payment method.

      Returns after installation

      If you change your mind, you can request a return within 30 days of your installation date. To qualify:

      • The panels must be undamaged and free of modifications.
      • Removal must be done by one of our installation partners. We'll book the appointment for you and restore the vehicle surface where the panels were mounted.
      • Your order reference or proof of purchase is required.

      Once the panels have been removed and inspected, we refund the full purchase price, including installation.

      What we can't accept for return

      • Panels damaged by an accident, misuse or improper cleaning
      • Systems installed, moved or repaired by someone other than an approved technician
      • Return requests made more than 30 days after installation (warranty coverage still applies)

      Refunds

      Refunds go back to your original payment method: credit or debit card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay. We process them within 10 business days of cancellation or removal. Your bank or provider may take a few extra days to show the refund.

      Defects and warranty

      Every system includes a one-year warranty from the installation date covering defects in materials and workmanship. If something isn't working as it should, contact us. We'll arrange an inspection and repair or replace the affected parts at no cost to you.

      How to start a return or cancellation

      1. Send us a message through the contact form and choose "Return or refund".
      2. Include your name, order reference and installation city.
      3. We'll reply within two business days with next steps and, for returns, a removal appointment.
